# Discovery questions

Ask these before the demo to pick the acts and the opening line. Each question names the act it shapes.

- Who answers a CloudWatch alarm on your team after hours, and how long until they are at a keyboard? (act 2)
- When an alarm fires, how often does the cause turn out to be a change somebody made in the console? (act 2)
- Do you run Terraform plans against production on a schedule, or only before a change? (act 1)
- How many AWS accounts does the team own, and how many people can read all of them? (act 1)
- Which IAM role would you give an engineer on their first day, and which one after a month? (acts 2 and 4)
- Where do you look today to see what an automated job changed in the account? (act 4)
- What does a product manager do today when they want a small change on an internal admin screen? (act 3)
- How long does a small internal change take from the ask to a URL someone can open? (act 3)
- Which chat or paging tool does the alarm reach first? The demo uses the Devin web app and a webhook, and the same Automation accepts other triggers. (act 2)
- Which queues or tables would you be nervous about anyone redriving or purging? (act 2)
